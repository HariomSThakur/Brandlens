# BrandLens short interview guide

## Status

No interviews have been conducted for this project yet (n=0). Do not describe this guide as completed research. Use it to conduct two or three short interviews, record the real sample size, and summarize answers without attributing private names unless participants consent.

## Recruit

Ask people who have created, reviewed, or approved marketing content. Record participant role only (for example, creator, reviewer, or team lead), not identifying details.

## Five questions

1. Walk me through how a piece of marketing content gets checked before it is published.
2. What is the hardest part of verifying a factual claim or finding its approved source?
3. How do you currently record edits, approvals, and reasons for decisions?
4. What would make an automated warning useful, and what would make you distrust it?
5. Looking at the BrandLens workflow, what is unclear or missing for you to use it without help?
